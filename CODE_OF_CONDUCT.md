# Code of Conduct

This is an educational community around industrial robotics. Be the person you'd want next to you at the pendant.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and patient — learners at every level are welcome, from first jog to integrator.
- Keep feedback about the content, not the person. "This line is wrong because…" beats "you clearly don't know…".
- Safety corrections are always welcome and always urgent. If a page or listing could mislead someone at a real cell, say so plainly and open an issue.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks. No spam or self-promotion unrelated to study material.
- Respect intellectual property: do not post FANUC or other OEM manual pages, courseware, or software. See [`LEGAL.md`](LEGAL.md).

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer, **Garry TJ**, through the repository. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may block repeat offenders.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
